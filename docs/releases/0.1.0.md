# Sea Glass 0.1.0

Release candidate notes. Maven Central availability has not been verified.

Sea Glass brings frosted, softly tumbled materials to Jetpack Compose and Compose Multiplatform. Version 0.1.0 targets Android 7.0+, desktop/JVM, ARM64 iOS devices, and Apple Silicon iOS simulators.

- Use `SeaGlassButton`, `SeaGlassSurface`, or `Modifier.seaGlass()` with native Compose content. Buttons include click semantics, keyboard focus, disabled state, and press feedback.
- Choose rounded, capsule, or disc shapes; control frost, grain, thickness, bulge, transmission, lighting, and elevation. Stable seeds preserve each piece’s contour and texture as colors or lighting change.
- Share defaults through `SeaGlassTheme` and reusable `SeaGlassComponentStyle` values. `SeaGlassMaterialTheme` adapts Material 3 colors across platforms; the Android helper supports wallpaper colors on Android 12+ with configurable fallbacks.
- Android 13+ and Skia render the complete material on the first draw. Lighting and color update immediately; rapid shape edits retain the last complete shape until the newest geometry is ready. Android 7–12L uses a cached asynchronous bitmap renderer.
- Explore recipes, palettes, and interactive examples in the adaptive Android and desktop playground.
- The live renderer packs geometry into half-precision textures once for Android and Skia uploads. Contour construction uses fewer temporary allocations, and a specialized horizontal distance transform preserves the reference field values.
- Repeatedly used geometry receives cache protection within the existing 4 MiB and 32-entry limits. The playground includes a deterministic 288-row list for checking scrolling, revisits, and accessible actions.

These changes reduce specific geometry, allocation, and retained-texture costs; they do not establish faster overall scrolling. The [allocation measurements](../performance/allocation-and-distance-transform.md), [long-list results](../performance/long-list-scrolling.md), and [render-cost investigation](../performance/render-cost-investigation.md) record the measured gains, regressions, and rejected experiments.

The material does not sample or distort content behind it. Allow room around controls for their outward glow, and check contrast with your content. Rendering differs across backends. iOS rendering, older-Android fallback behavior on devices, and physical-device frame timing remain unverified.

The public API is still evolving during `0.x`. Patch releases remain compatible; minor releases may include breaking changes with migration notes. See the [release process](../releasing.md).
